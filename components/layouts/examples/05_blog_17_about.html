Our Journey

How DevBlog grew over the years

2020

DevBlog Launched

Started as a personal blog by Sarah Chen, publishing first articles about React and JavaScript.

2021

First Contributors Join

Expanded to 5 authors, launched categories system, and published first series on TypeScript.

2022

Added video content to complement written tutorials. YouTube channel reached 50K subscribers.
